## [](#17-代碼分析工具)17\. 代碼分析工具

## [](#18-補充和推薦資源)18\. 補充和推薦資源

-   [重構](https://book.douban.com/subject/1229923/) 這本書是軟體開發領域的經典之作，原作使用 Java 代碼，新出的[第二版](https://www.tenlong.com.tw/products/9789865021832)改用 JavaScript。另有 [Ruby 版本](https://www.amazon.com/Refactoring-Ruby-Addison-Wesley-Professional/dp/0321984137)但沒有中文翻譯。
-   [how DHH organizes his rails controllers](http://jeromedalbert.com/how-dhh-organizes-his-rails-controllers/) Rails 發明人 DHH 如何組織 Controller 代碼，堅持 RESTful
-   [Put chubby models on a diet with concerns](https://signalvnoise.com/posts/3372-put-chubby-models-on-a-diet-with-concerns) DHH 對於 concerns 用法的看法

當你仍不滿足 Rails 的 concerns 機制時，你會需要更多物件導向的知識，在程式語言教程中有介紹到。關於 Rails 的部分推薦以下補充資料：

-   [7 Patterns to Refactor Fat ActiveRecord Models](http://blog.codeclimate.com/blog/2012/10/17/7-ways-to-decompose-fat-activerecord-models/)
-   [Object Oriented Rails – Writing better controllers](https://content.pivotal.io/blog/object-oriented-rails-writing-better-controllers)
-   [Slimming Down Your Models and Controllers with Concerns, Service Objects, and Tableless Models](https://www.viget.com/articles/slimming-down-your-models-and-controllers)
-   [Growing Rails Applications in Practice](https://leanpub.com/growing-rails)
