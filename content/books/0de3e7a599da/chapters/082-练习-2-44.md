`up-split` 的定义和 `right-split` 的定义非常相似，唯一的区别是 `beside` 和 `below` 的位置不同：

;;; 44-up-split.scm

(define (up-split painter n)
    (if (\= n 0)
        painter
        (let ((smaller (up-split painter (\- n 1))))
            (below painter
                   (beside smaller smaller)))))
