## 向量的表示[¶](#id2 "Permalink to this headline")

定义：

;;; 46-vect-represent.scm

(define (make-vect xcor ycor)
    (list xcor ycor))

(define (xcor-vect v)
    (car v))

(define (ycor-vect v)
    (cadr v))

## add-vect[¶](#add-vect "Permalink to this headline")

将公式 \\((x\_1, y\_1) + (x\_2, y\_2) = (x\_1 + x\_2, y\_1 + y\_2)\\) 写成相应的函数：

;;; 46-add-vect.scm

(load "46-vect-represent.scm")

(define (add-vect vect another-vect)
    (make-vect (\+ (xcor-vect vect)
                  (xcor-vect another-vect))
               (\+ (ycor-vect vect)
                  (ycor-vect another-vect))))

## scale-vect[¶](#scale-vect "Permalink to this headline")

根据公式 \\(s \\cdot (x, y) = (sx, sy)\\) 写出相应的函数：

;;; 46-scale-vect.scm

(load "46-vect-represent.scm")

(define (scale-vect factor vect)
    (make-vect (\* factor (xcor-vect vect))
               (\* factor (ycor-vect vect))))
