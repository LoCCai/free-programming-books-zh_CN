    Work on dependent features without losing track.

    ### Advanced Custom Workflows
    
    Start and finish topic branches in a couple of clicks.
    
    Starting creates a correctly-named topic branch and checks it out immediately; finishing merges it into its parent and deletes the fully-merged branch right away.
    
    Learn more](https://www.git-tower.com/pro/advanced-custom-workflows)

    ### Graphite Workflow
    
    The Graphite stack workflow, without the CLI.
    
    Create, restack, and submit Graphite-style stacked PRs directly from Tower. Authenticate once and let Tower drive Graphite for every branch and stack in your workflow.
    
    Learn more](https://www.git-tower.com/pro/graphite-workflow)

    ### Automatic Branch Management
    
    Clean up stale and merged branches in one action.
    
    A dedicated Archive Branches dialog lists every merged and stale candidate in one place. Archive in batches, restore on demand, or enable fully automatic cleanup with per-branch opt-outs.
    
    Learn more](https://www.git-tower.com/pro/automatic-branch-management)
