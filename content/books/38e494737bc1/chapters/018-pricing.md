## Frequently Asked Questions

##### How does the free trial work?

Download our latest version and try Tower completely free for 30 days - no credit card required! The trial includes all features of Tower Pro.

##### Why isn't there a free plan?

We'd rather give you the full, unrestricted version of Tower to properly evaluate than a stripped-down free tier. That's why every plan starts with a 30-day free trial - no credit card required - so you can try all of Tower Pro before you decide.

##### How does the subscription work?

Tower is offered as a standard subscription: you'll have access to Tower (including updates and support) as long as your subscription is active / paid for. If you cancel your subscription, access to Tower ends when the most recently paid-up subscription period ends.

##### What's your refund policy?

We try to keep refunds simple for our users. If you purchased Tower and changed your mind, let us know within 30 days and we'll be happy to refund you. No questions asked! Even better, test Tower first with our 30-day free trial.

##### Where's the license agreement?

You can read and review our [license agreement](https://www.git-tower.com/legal/eula) at any time.

##### Can I still use an old version?

If you've purchased a license for Tower for Mac (v1 or v2) or Tower for Windows (v1), you can of course keep using this version! In case you need an old version, you can [download](https://www.git-tower.com/release-notes) it here.

##### Does my license work on both platforms?

Your license works cross-platform, on both macOS and Windows.

##### Do I get an upgrade discount?

If you purchased a license for Tower for Mac (v1 or v2) or Tower for Windows (v1) before, you'll receive a 50% discount on your first year with the new Tower - for any number of seats. Simply keep your old license key ready during checkout.

##### Is Tower free for students?

If you are enrolled as a student, you can use Tower for **free**! [Learn more and apply!](https://www.git-tower.com/students)

##### Is Tower free for schools?

Educational institutions and their staff members as well as non-profit organizations can use Tower for **free**! [Learn more and apply!](https://www.git-tower.com/education)

##### Reviewing Tower for press?

If you would like to review Tower, you can [download our press kit](https://www.git-tower.com/company/press) including logos, screenshots and more.
