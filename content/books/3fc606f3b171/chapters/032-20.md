The `PSDraw` module provides simple print support for Postscript printers. You can print text, graphics and images through this module.

_class_ `PIL.PSDraw.``PSDraw`(_fp=None_)[\[源代码\]](https://pillow-docs-cn.readthedocs.io/zh_CN/latest/_modules/PIL/PSDraw.html#PSDraw)[¶](#PIL.PSDraw.PSDraw "永久链接至目标")

Sets up printing to the given file. If **fp** is omitted, `sys.stdout` is assumed.

`begin_document`(_id=None_)[\[源代码\]](https://pillow-docs-cn.readthedocs.io/zh_CN/latest/_modules/PIL/PSDraw.html#PSDraw.begin_document)[¶](#PIL.PSDraw.PSDraw.begin_document "永久链接至目标")

Set up printing of a document. (Write Postscript DSC header.)

`end_document`()[\[源代码\]](https://pillow-docs-cn.readthedocs.io/zh_CN/latest/_modules/PIL/PSDraw.html#PSDraw.end_document)[¶](#PIL.PSDraw.PSDraw.end_document "永久链接至目标")

Ends printing. (Write Postscript DSC footer.)

`image`(_box_, _im_, _dpi=None_)[\[源代码\]](https://pillow-docs-cn.readthedocs.io/zh_CN/latest/_modules/PIL/PSDraw.html#PSDraw.image)[¶](#PIL.PSDraw.PSDraw.image "永久链接至目标")

Draw a PIL image, centered in the given box.

`line`(_xy0_, _xy1_)[\[源代码\]](https://pillow-docs-cn.readthedocs.io/zh_CN/latest/_modules/PIL/PSDraw.html#PSDraw.line)[¶](#PIL.PSDraw.PSDraw.line "永久链接至目标")

Draws a line between the two points. Coordinates are given in Postscript point coordinates (72 points per inch, (0, 0) is the lower left corner of the page).
