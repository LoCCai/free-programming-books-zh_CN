**HTML <pre>** 元素表示预定义格式文本。在该元素中的文本通常按照原文件中的编排，以等宽字体的形式展现出来，文本中的空白符（比如空格和换行符）都会显示出来。(紧跟在 <pre> 开始标签后的换行符也会被省略)

## [属性](#属性)

这个元素只具有全局属性。

[`cols`](#cols)

定义每行的最大字符数。这是一个非标准的属性，作用与[`width`](#width)相同。要实现这样的效果，应该使用 CSS。

[`width`](#width)

包含每行的最大字符数。虽然在技术上仍被实现，但这个属性没有视觉效果。要实现这样的效果，应该使用 CSS。

[`wrap`](#wrap)

提示溢出怎样发生。在现代浏览器中，这个提示会被忽略，且被没有视觉效果。要实现这样的效果，应该使用 CSS。

## [无障碍](#无障碍)

It is important to provide an alternate description for any images or diagrams created using preformatted text. The alternate description should clearly and concisely describe the image or diagram's content.

People experiencing low vision conditions and browsing with the aid of assistive technology such as a screen reader may not understand what the preformatted text characters are representing when they are read out in sequence.

A combination of the [`<figure>`](https://developer.mozilla.org/zh-CN/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/figure) and [`<figcaption>`](https://developer.mozilla.org/zh-CN/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/figcaption) elements, supplemented by a combination of an [`id`](https://developer.mozilla.org/zh-CN/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Global_attributes#id) and the [ARIA](https://developer.mozilla.org/zh-CN/docs/Web/Accessibility/ARIA) `role` and `aria-labelledby` attributes allow the preformatted text to be announced as an image, with the `figcaption` serving as the image's alternate description.

### [示例](#示例_2)

html

```
<figure role="img" aria-labelledby="cow-caption">
  <pre>
  ___________________________
< I'm an expert in my field. >
  ---------------------------
         \   ^__^
          \  (oo)\_______
             (__)\       )\/\
                 ||----w |
                 ||     ||
  </pre>
  <figcaption id="cow-caption">
    A cow saying, "I'm an expert in my field." The cow is illustrated using
    preformatted text characters.
  </figcaption>
</figure>
```
