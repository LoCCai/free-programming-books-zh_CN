# 定义关联

> 译者：[飞龙](https://github.com/wizardforcel)

> 来源：[Defining Associations](https://github.com/dresende/node-orm2/wiki/Defining-Associations)

关联是一个或多个[模型之间](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wizardforcel/orm2-doc-zh-cn/HEAD/3. Defining Models.md)的关系。

关联的类型：

+ [hasOne（多对一）](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wizardforcel/orm2-doc-zh-cn/HEAD/4.1 hasOne.md)
+ [hasMany（多对多）](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wizardforcel/orm2-doc-zh-cn/HEAD/4.2 hasMany.md)
+ [extendsTo（一对一）](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wizardforcel/orm2-doc-zh-cn/HEAD/4.3 extendsTo.md)
