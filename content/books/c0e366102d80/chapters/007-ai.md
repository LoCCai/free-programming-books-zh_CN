### Built with trust in mind

Nothing moves forward without your team’s green light, keeping AI support fully secure.

Human-in-the-Loop approval

For every generative or action-oriented command (e.g., creating an issue), the user must manually approve the AI's proposed action, ensuring quality control and preventing errors.

![Human-in-the-Loop approval](https://nulab.com/nuweb_assets/images/backlog/ai/security-1.webp)

Access control

All AI retrieval and action capabilities strictly adhere to the user’s existing access permissions (per project, per user).

![Access control](https://nulab.com/nuweb_assets/images/backlog/ai/security-2.webp)

Audit logs

All AI-driven creation, update, or deletion actions are clearly marked in the audit log, ensuring traceability and compliance
