## Our benefits & perks

-   ### Competitive salary
    
    We bring in the best talent, so we have no problem paying every employee what they deserve.
    
-   ### Full health coverage
    
    We want our employees happy and healthy. That means fully paid health, vision, and dental starting your first day on the job.
    
-   ### Paid vacation
    
    From your first year, you can expect 20 vacation days and 5 sick days to use whenever your heart desires.
    
-   ### Team happy hours
    
    Team outings, video calls, and local events allow us to bond as a team and celebrate wins together.
    
-   ### Free books
    
    Whether you want to learn new professional skills or brush up on old ones, we’ve got you covered with the latest resources out there.
    
-   ### Pick your machine
    
    Mac, PC... Can't we all just get along? Use whatever machine makes you comfortable and productive. Workers choice.
