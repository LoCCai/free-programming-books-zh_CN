### 5\. Quick access to your domain

In most cases access to the domain will be available within one to two hours of purchase, however access to domains purchased after business hours will be available within the next business day. We create an account for you at the domain's current registrar, [NameBright.com](https://www.hugeDomains.com/namebright.cfm), where you'll be able to manage the domain.

If you're purchasing the domain outright, you'll be able to transfer the domain to any registrar — remember that we cannot accept returns on domains that have been transferred. If you finance the domain with a payment plan, you can still manage the domain, but it will have a registrar lock preventing the transfer until the payment plan is completed.
